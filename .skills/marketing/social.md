# Social & Community

## Rules

1. Social is for the **beachhead's** platforms only (from `market-research.md` "where they gather") — being everywhere = being nowhere; posting without engagement = billboard in a desert
2. Every channel gets a JOB (choose): support/deflection, thought-leadership, distribution, community-building, recruitment — a channel without a job gets closed, not maintained out of guilt

## Social Workflow

1. Channel selection: max 2 active channels initially (evidence-based pick); declare each channel's job + the metric it moves (followers ≠ goal; the metric must tie to `business/growth.md` North-star or support load)
2. Content ratio per week (rule of thirds): value/education 60% · community/engagement 30% · product/promo 10% — promo-heavy feeds die algorithmically and socially
3. Cadence: sustainable and consistent > heroic and sporadic (3 solid posts/week beats 20 then silence)
4. Derivative-first: social posts derive from content pieces (`marketing/content.md` distribution rule) — social is a megaphone for the engine, not a separate engine
5. Engagement protocol: respond to every relevant comment/mention within 24h (the actual "social" part — broadcast-only accounts are ads with extra steps); guidelines: helpful, honest, never argue publicly — take disputes to DM/support
6. Measurement: per-channel job metric (support deflection, referrals, reach in beachhead) — monthly review, kill underperforming channels (document in growth log)

## Community Workflow (if the beachhead gathers in communities)

1. Pick communities from research evidence (the top 1-2 where the segment actually is)
2. **90-10 rule**: 90% genuinely helpful participation (answers, resources, no links), 10% our content — and only when it genuinely answers the question
3. Never astroturf (fake accounts/shill replies) — exposed astroturfing kills brand trust permanently; recorded as banned tactic per `business/growth.md` no-dark-patterns
4. Build OWN community only when: existing ones are saturated with us + users organically cluster (signs: users helping users unprompted) — premature communities are ghost towns; start with a single purpose (space where users answer each other = support deflection + retention signals)

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] ≤ 2 channels, each with declared job + tied metric
- [ ] 60/30/10 ratio followed; engagement SLA held
- [ ] Community participation 90-10; zero astroturfing
